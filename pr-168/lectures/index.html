OpenEMR Lecture Series

The OpenEMR Development Lecture Series is a once monthly lecture on a OpenEMR Development topic on the second Saturday of every month. Anyone interested in listening and participating is welcome.

The Day
The second Saturday of every month
The Time
2:00pm Eastern / 11:00 am Pacific

  • Medical Billing Clearinghouse and Eligibility Verification in OpenEMR

    Brad Sharp
    Saturday, May 20, 2023

    Brad Sharp demonstrates the ClaimRev Clearinghouse Module, which is an easy to use OpenEMR Module that supports Medical Billing Clearinghouse and Eligibility Verification.

  • First Impressions Are Everything: Creating an Inviting OpenEMR Login Page for your Users

    Robert Down
    Saturday, February 18, 2023

    Robert Down discusses how to customize the OpenEMR login page.

  • Twig, Macros, and Partials: Streamline your OpenEMR Development

    Robert Down
    Saturday, February 18, 2023

    Robert Down discusses how to streamline your OpenEMR developement with twig, macros, and partials.

  • C-CDA and OpenEMR - Everything you need to know

    Jerry Padgett
    Saturday, January 14, 2023

    Jerry Padgett discusses everything you need to know about C-CDA (Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture) for OpenEMR.

  • VisualEHR (VEHR) Timeline Based Dashboard Module in OpenEMR

    John Purdy from Omega Systems Group, Inc.
    Saturday, December 10, 2022

    Omega Systems Group describes and demonstrates a timeline based dashboard module in OpenEMR that they are nearing completion on.

  • OpenEMR Form Builder: New Feature - Quick and Easy Form Builder

    Jerry Padgett
    Saturday, November 12, 2022

    Jerry Padgett describes a new quick and easy form builder for your questionnaires/assessments that can be used for both encounter forms and patient portal forms. This new feature allows quick use of already existent standardized LOINC forms in addition to forms built via the NLM Form Builder. Because of the poor audio in this video, a chat is also included which transcribes the video.

  • Customizing OpenEMR For Your Practice Using Layout Based Forms

    Sandra Gutierrez, MD
    Saturday, April 9, 2022

    Learn how to customize OpenEMR for your practice using Layout Based Forms in this lecture and demonstration by Sandra Gutierrez. Sandra has experience in using Layout Based Forms in her own practice.

  • Acute Care Clinical Workflow

    Robert Down, RN
    Saturday, March 5, 2022

    Learn about the daily workflow of acute care clinical staff in this lecture by Robert Down, who currently works as a travel nurse. Robert has experience in direct-patient care and leadership of nursing units.

  • OpenEMR Patient Portal Presentation: New Features - Patient Form Templates Management

    Jerry Padgett
    Saturday, February 12, 2022

    Jerry Padgett describes new features in Patient Portal, which include patient form templates management. Overview on creating and managing patient form templates and distributing them with Template Profiles and Patient Groups.

  • OpenEMR FHIR Lecture Series: Lecture 2 - FHIR Bulk Data in OpenEMR

    Stephen Nielson
    Saturday, January 15, 2022

    Stephen Nielson describes OpenEMR’s FHIR Bulk Data API

  • OpenEMR FHIR Lecture Series: Lecture 1 - Overview and Use of FHIR in OpenEMR

    Stephen Nielson
    Saturday, January 8, 2022

    Stephen Nielson describes OpenEMR’s FHIR API

  • OpenEMR Modules

    Stephen Nielson
    Saturday, November 20, 2021

    Stephen Nielson describes how to effectively develop OpenEMR modules

  • Event Driven Architecture

    Robert Down, BSN, RN
    Saturday, November 13, 2021

    Robert Down discusses Event Listeners and how to leverage Event Listeners in OpenEMR development
